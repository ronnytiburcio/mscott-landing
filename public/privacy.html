Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how M.Scott Consulting LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our website and the tools and services we operate, including features that connect to your Pinterest account through the Pinterest API.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Information you provide. Your name, email address, and any details you share when you contact us, book a consultation, or request our services.
  • Pinterest account data. When you choose to connect your Pinterest account, we access certain data through the Pinterest API only with your authorization. Depending on the features you use, this may include your Pinterest profile information, boards, pins, and pin or account analytics. We only request the access (scopes) needed to provide the features you ask for.
  • Usage and device data. Basic technical information such as browser type, device, and pages viewed, collected automatically when you visit our website.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve our services and the features you request;
  • Create, schedule, analyze, or manage Pinterest content on your behalf when you direct us to;
  • Respond to your inquiries and communicate with you about our services;
  • Maintain the security and integrity of our services and comply with legal obligations.

3. Pinterest Data Use & Compliance

Our access to and use of Pinterest data is limited to providing the specific features you authorize. We handle Pinterest data in accordance with the Pinterest Developer Guidelines, the Pinterest Developer Agreement, and Pinterest's applicable policies. We do not sell Pinterest data, and we do not use it for advertising or any purpose unrelated to the features you have requested. We only retain Pinterest data for as long as needed to provide those features, or as required by law.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers. With trusted vendors who help us operate our services (for example, hosting and analytics providers), who are bound to protect the information and use it only on our behalf.
  • Third-party platforms you connect. With platforms you choose to use through us, such as Pinterest, to perform the actions you request.
  • Legal reasons. When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of our users or others.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information and Pinterest data only as long as necessary to provide our services, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or meet legal requirements. When data is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

6. Revoking Access

You can disconnect your Pinterest account from our services at any time. You may also revoke our access directly from your Pinterest account settings under "Apps" or connected applications. Once access is revoked, we stop accessing your Pinterest data and delete stored Pinterest data that is no longer required, except where retention is required by law.

7. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Some features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for business users and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below and we will respond as required by applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected on this page, and your continued use of our services after the changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
